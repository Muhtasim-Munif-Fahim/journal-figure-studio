# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] as its
code of conduct. The full text is at that link and governs in case of any
difference with the summary below.

In short: everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any project space is expected to behave professionally and
without harassment. Concretely, that means

- assume good faith and give feedback on the work, not the person;
- accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and that a contributor may
  decline to do more work on it;
- do not harass anyone, publicly or privately, including through unwanted
  contact, sexualized attention, insults, or demeaning remarks about someone's
  identity or background;
- do not publish others' private information;
- do not derail threads with off-topic conflict.

Academic software attracts strong opinions about method. Disagreement about the
right statistical practice, figure convention, or venue rule is welcome and
expected; contempt for the people holding the other position is not.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and releases — and when someone is representing the project in
public, such as posting about it under the project's name.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to **s1911024120@ru.ac.bd** with `CONDUCT` in the
subject line. Reports go to the project maintainer, Muhtasim Munif Fahim
(@Muhtasim-Munif-Fahim).

You should expect an acknowledgement within 5 business days. Reports are kept
confidential; the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the reported person
without the reporter's agreement.

This project has a single maintainer, which has an obvious limit: if your
concern is about the maintainer, there is no internal appeal. In that case you
may raise it with [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/), which can act
on Terms of Service violations independently of this repository.

## Consequences

The maintainer may, in proportion to the conduct: give a private warning; edit
or delete the offending comment with a public note that it was removed; close a
thread; temporarily block an account from the repository; or permanently ban an
account. Bans are documented in the enforcement record, not announced publicly
except where a public correction is needed.

Enforcement decisions follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines — correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
